I am an Assistant Professor in the [School of Mathematics and Statistics](https://www.ucd.ie/mathstat/) at University College Dublin. Previously, I was a postdoctoral researcher at the Bocconi Institute for Data Science and Analytics ([BIDSA](https://www.bidsa.unibocconi.eu/wps/wcm/connect/Site/Bidsa/Home)), where I worked with [Giacomo Zanella](https://sites.google.com/site/gzanellawebpage/home). I obtained my PhD in Statistical Sciences at the University of Padova, where I was advised by [Bruno Scarpa](https://homes.stat.unipd.it/brunoscarpa/), [Daniele Durante](https://danieledurante.github.io/web/) and [Botond Szabo](https://botondszabo.com/research-team/).

Main research interests
======
* Bayesian computation, in particular Markov chain Monte Carlo, deterministic posterior approximations, and the interplay between them.
* Bayesian asymptotics, with a focus on higher order theory.
* Bias reduction methods for small sample parametric inference.
* Biostatistical methodology.
